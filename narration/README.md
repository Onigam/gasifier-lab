# Narration scripts (French and English)

`fr.json` and `en.json` follow `lab-kit/docs/narration.md`: **73 segments each** (`intro`, 5 worlds, 67 parts: farm 10, tractor 13, gasifier 20, gas line 17, garage 7), ids `intro`, `scene-<world>`, `part-<id>`, no lesson segments. The audio is in the **local voice** (`--engine voicebox`, profiles `Onigam narrateur FR` and `EN`, 0 credit, no ElevenLabs), in `public/audio/<lang>/` (11 MB a language, 73 mp3 and a manifest): see « Generated and checked » below. **Nobody has listened to it**: what is measured is below, and what could only be heard is said there.

`npm run build` runs `scripts/check-narration.mjs` first: every segment of a manifest needs its file, and a manifest and its script must name the same segments. `npm test` holds the scripts (`src/narration.test.ts`), the manifests and the guards of the refused sizes.

Check the length without generating anything (`HOME=<empty dir>` keeps the tool from even opening a key file):

```
HOME=$(mktemp -d) node ~/Dev/lab-kit/tools/narration/generate.mjs --src narration/fr.json --out public/audio/fr --dry-run --engine voicebox
HOME=$(mktemp -d) node ~/Dev/lab-kit/tools/narration/generate.mjs --src narration/en.json --out public/audio/en --dry-run --engine voicebox
```

## Register

Pelton Lab's tone (`lab-kit/docs/narration-style.md`). The thread is **one wood chip**, as the research asked: it is born under the chipper's knives, rides the roof rack, sinks for an hour through the four zones, and is left as a little ash while its gas goes on to the engine. It is in the introduction and in the gasifier's world; the parts keep to what each does, a surprise first and a concrete detail last (`injection-pump`: the gasifier is hot; `throat-ring`: « pas comment la faire »; `old-rescue-set`: « à quel point c'était courant »). The introduction closes on « une plaquette met une heure à descendre » and not on « à vous de jouer »: the last world is about a gas that kills.

**What the voice does not say, by the lead's rules.** The carbon monoxide warning, the emergency numbers and the first-aid line are not in the voice at all: the entry notice, the Sources panel and the garage's overview say them, once each. No sentence tells how to build, light, run or make safe a gasifier, and `OPERATING_FR` / `OPERATING_EN` of `src/data/lint.ts` are run on every spoken sentence. The words that promise (`PROMISES`) and the sentences of the sources the lab does not repeat (`NOT_REPEATED`) are held off too.

**The model's figures.** « 34 kg an hour before 41 »: every segment that says the bench's forty-one says the model's thirty-four before it, and a test holds the order. The voice never says that the model retrieves the machine: it says « pourquoi cet écart ? » and gives the sheet's reason. It never says the model's flame goes out (a test holds every « s'éteint » and « goes out » to the lamp that never does, and to the flame that never does). « Le modèle » is in 18 segments of 73, where a figure stands against the bench's or the book's, never on every figure (a test holds 15 to 36).

**Doors.** Nowhere does the voice say that a door, an open window or the open air helps. Where the open-door cue runs (`scene-garage`, `garage-door`), the Swedes' verdict (« très insuffisantes ») is spoken before the cue, and the figure that falls is said to be what a well-mixed box is built to say; a test refuses an open-door cue with no verdict before it.

**Anonymous.** « un institut suédois », « le rapport », « le livre suédois », « un autre manuel », « une source américaine »: no person, no company, no brand (the tractor, its engine and the truck are not named), no institution by its name, no city, no clinic, no treatment's name. A test refuses every name of the Sources panel and of the sheets (`NAMES` in `src/narration.test.ts`). The Sources panel says who said what, and the introduction says so once.

**The sizes of the hot parts.** None, in any language, and none hinted. The voice says what the throat ring, the nozzles, the fire box, the grate and the zones do, their lives (« sept cents heures », « six cents à mille heures », « quatre cents à sept cents heures ») and their temperatures, and counts neither nozzles nor zones' heights. `src/data/spoken.ts` reads the number words of a script back as digits (« deux mètres vingt-deux » is « 2 m 22 »), and `src/data/docs-guard.test.ts` and `src/data/refusals.test.ts` hold every printed form of every refused length off both scripts, in both languages, and off any sentence that names a hot part and a length. Both guards were broken on purpose, once, to see them fail.

## Cues (160 in each language, the same in both)

- Totals: `lab` 60 (preset 18, journey 22, scenario 12, speed 3, door 2, moisture, place and engine 1 each), frame 35, flows 26, home 11, xray 10, explode 6, mode 6, scene 5, select 1.
- Introduction (27): `home`, then the chip's trip: `frame` the barn and the chipper, `scene` to the tractor, `frame` the rack, `scene` to the gasifier with five `lab:journey` stops (drying, pyrolysis, flame, reduction, ash), `scene` to the gas line with two `frame`, `scene` to the garage with the engine's stop (`lab:scenario:engine-stops`) and `frame` the plume, the preset back, `scene` to the farm, the `mode` hops (assembly, installation, simulation, lessons, drawings, explore), `select:none`.
- Worlds (12, 13, 19, 10, 12): `home` first, one `frame` per element named (the garage frames four of its seven parts, the others have their own segment), `flows` where a path is told, `xray` and `explode` in the tractor's world only, `lab:journey` in the gasifier's. The farm uses no `xray` (it needs a selection there) and no `explode` (its tool is off); no world asks for a section, and none has one.
- Parts: no camera cue (a part starts framed and selected). `flows` on `gas-line-run`, `condensate-sleeve`, `air-nozzles`, `gas-outlet`, `hot-gas-pipe`, `primary-air-valve`, `gas-cooler`, `closed-garage`; `xray` on `cab`, `gasifier-unit`, `outer-casing`, `filter-box` (the parts that open their world's shell: a test refuses an X-ray on any other); `explode` on `tractor-chassis`, `eccentric-lock`.
- **The simulation follows the voice** (`src/cues.ts`): `lab:journey` on `chip-column`, `drying-zone`, `pyrolysis-zone`, `oxidation-zone`, `reduction-zone`, `ash-pit` (the cursor stands where the voice is); `lab:moisture:40` on `drying-zone`; `lab:scenario:air-leak` on `eccentric-lock`; `lab:scenario:filter-ageing` on `filter-bags` and `water-gauge`; `lab:scenario:idling` on `gas-throttle`; `lab:scenario:engine-stops` on `front-wheels` and `post-gas-plume`; `lab:scenario:closed-garage` on `closed-garage`, `garage-door` (with `lab:door:open`), `room-above` and `co-indicator` (with `lab:speed:600`); `lab:place:garage` and `lab:engine:off` on `exhaust-plume`.
- **The simulation is given back.** Every segment that touches it ends with `lab:preset:mf-1100` (the drawn machine, as the preset gives it) or leaves the journey on its own default (0.62): a test runs the cues of every segment on a real `GasifierSim` and compares its parameters with a fresh one's. `scene-gasifier`, `scene-gas-train`, `intro` and `exhaust-plume` also open with the preset, so that a visitor who had changed the run hears them on the machine they speak of.
- Spacing: camera cues at least 35 spoken characters apart, counted on the spoken text as the generator counts it (cues out, runs of spaces made one). Everything an `xray`, `flows` or `explode` cue switches on is switched off before the end of its segment.

## Spoken form

Numbers and units in words, « parties par million » for ppm, « mégajoules par mètre cube » and « kilojoules » in full, no symbol, no abbreviation, « à l'écran » for what the sheets call the 3D, acronyms replaced by words (« monoxyde de carbone », never CO). Estimates are said « environ » and « une estimation » where the sheet stars them, and the model's results are said « le modèle » where a figure stands against the bench's; nothing is hedged twice. English: British spelling and units (metres, litres, tyres, storey, programme, grey), « per cent ».

## French and English

English is an adaptation: the same ids, the same cues at the matching place (a test holds every cue within a quarter of the segment of its French place), shorter by 5 per cent. A few turns are English ones: « il mange du bois » is « it eats wood », « c'est l'histoire la plus grave de ce lab » is « the gravest story in this lab », « une pièce où l'on vit » is « a room where people live ». « Voyez » is « watch » or « see »; « le lab » is « the lab ». Nothing in the French is France-only, so nothing had to be made general: « voyez votre autorité locale » is « ask your local authority ».

## Left out on purpose

See `docs/narration-notes.md`: the nine pellet-store deaths, the legal limits of carbon monoxide and the alarms' levels, the deaths of 1939 to 1941 and the defective gasifiers of 1945, « sometimes for hours » as a quotation, the names of the old treatments and of the clinic, the casing's two losses as numbers, the sheets' lines on where the black water must not go. All of them stand on sources read in summary or on disposal wording that the lead keeps off the voice.

The Whisper gaps are all of four kinds: a homophone, a unit or a number written another way, a rare word the small model does not know (« déchiqueteuse », « brise-mottes », « goudrons »), and the lines it invents over silence. No passage is missing, doubled or cut in any clip.

**The probe.** `window.__lab.narration` (the kit's own engine, `expose` in dev) was driven in headless Chrome at 1280 by 800 on a dev server, in both languages: for each of the 73 segments the guide is played, seeked to just after every cue with the clock held, and the world, the mode, the selected part, the X-ray, the explode, the flows and the simulation's own state are read and compared with what the cue asks, the end of the segment being played out so that the markers left after the last word run. Every part segment starts in its world with its part selected. **Result, both languages: 73 segments, 139 cue checks (the 21 others are the markers that stand at the very end of a segment or at the same instant as another of the lab's cues, which the end-of-segment and last-of-the-group readings cover), 0 problems.** Nothing is left on at the end of a segment, and the simulation is back on the drawn machine (preset, scenario none, door shut, place field, engine on, one minute a second, journey 0.62, moisture 8). The probe found three faults of its own, none of the guide's: a reload of the dev server when a source file was saved, the end of a clip that must be played out rather than seeked to, and two cues at the same instant.

**What nobody could check.** Nobody has listened: the pronunciation of « tuyères », « goudrons », « déchiqueteuse », « kilojoules », « gasifier », « phenols »; the rhythm of the pauses at « … »; whether the voice sounds like the other labs' narrator; whether a subtitle falls on the word it shows. Whisper hears words, not intonation, and the voice may stress a number or the end of a sentence in a way no transcript shows. The camera's flights and the simulation's motion were looked at on a few screenshots (the garage's plume after the stop, the journey's cursor, the X-ray on the cab) and not on a real device.
