# Water, gas and smoke: what follows the model and what is decoration

No water the physics speaks of is open in this lab: the one free surface in the model's own numbers is the condensate in two jars, and it follows the model. Gas and smoke take the place of water and follow the same rule (`lab-quality.md` §3): where the physics says something the picture obeys it, and what is decoration is listed here. Every motion takes `view.slowMo`, and `dt` = 0 moves nothing.
