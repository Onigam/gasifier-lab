# Lessons of the Gasifier Lab

Seven lessons, two questions each (fourteen, over the bar of six and twelve), every one opening on a world, a part and a scenario of the simulation, and naming others, so that together they reach all twelve scenarios (`src/data/lessons.ts`, `src/data/lessons.test.ts`). The order is the research's (§0.6): the match, the gas that kills, the journey, the engine, the dirt, the energy, the day. The second is the lab's heart: the most dangerous minute of the machine is the one where it stops.

## What the lessons say about the model

- **34 kg an hour, then 41.** The wood the model burns at the bench's maximum is said first, then what the bench measured (§3.3 of `docs/physics.md`): the model does not retrieve the machine, and no lesson says it does. A lesson never says that the flame goes out in the model: it does not.
- **The tar is carried, not predicted.** `tarCrackedShare` is a shape, never a result; the measured 0.13 g/Nm³ at full power is the number a lesson prints.
- **The room is an estimate of an unmeasured source.** The 1 m³ the stop leaves is nobody's measure, the buildings are imagined, the model's room mixes perfectly. No lesson says a door or a larger room helps, that twenty minutes is when it ends, or how long a person has. The warning is said in full once, in the garage's overview and the Sources panel; the lessons name the danger and do not repeat it.
- **The engine.** 55 kW against 51 measured agrees by luck: the book's efficiencies move it from 47 to 60 kW, and the same model reads 30 % high on the other engines (the MF 1100 was the best of the fleet per litre).
- **The day.** The report estimates 15 to 25 minutes of service per hour; the durations of its own tables give 13 in the model (PL-G04 adds them by hand: 12 to 19). The gap is said, not closed.
- **The energy.** The bench's figure comes first (221 kW in, 40 out), and the model's wood, 34 kg an hour against 41, is said in the same breath, with its two reasons (the model's engine burns better than the bench's, and so does its gasifier: `docs/physics.md` §3.3; the bench's tractor burned about a third more than a good diesel). The misprint of the part-load line is shown and the lab says it takes the curve; nothing is set to the printed figure.
- **No lesson tells how to build, light, run or make safe any part.** The throat is explained and not sized.

## Tests

`src/data/lessons.test.ts`: seven lessons, two questions each, three choices of about the same length with the right answer neither always in one place nor told by its length (the longest right in about one question in three), the answer in range, every scenario, world and part real and in the same world, all twelve scenarios reachable from a lesson, four paragraphs of at most 95 words, figures as chips with their marks. `src/data/data.test.ts` holds the visitor texts' rules on the lessons too (parity in both languages, marks, no research code, no promise of safety, no size of the hot parts).
