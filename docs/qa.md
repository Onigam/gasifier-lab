## Defects

No blocker. Counts: **0 blocker, 7 major, 10 minor**. The majors are ranked: D1, D2 and D6 are against the lab's own refusals, D3 to D5 against what a visitor can read, D7 is a judgement. The fact-check's findings (next but one section) are not counted here.

### Major

**D1. The fuel casing's sheet prints a size the lab says it withholds (R2).** Where: `gasifier/fuel-casing`, tab Caractéristiques, the row « Diamètre × hauteur de la soute » (EN « Bunker diameter × height »), in both languages. Its first figure is the bunker's diameter, which equals, to the millimetre, the diameter of the lower casing that `GASIFIER_REFUSED` and `src/data/refusals.test.ts` withhold. The guard lets it pass only because the number is printed as « a × b m ». A reader who sees that the 3D draws both casings about as wide as each other has the lower casing's width, which is the figure the lab withholds, and the throat's sheet itself says that size is « la chose dont un constructeur aurait le plus besoin ». The narrator flagged it (`docs/narration-notes.md`); my judgement is that it is a breach, not an oddity, and that it is one the lead must settle before the owner reads it: either the row loses its first figure (the bunker is also given by its volume, 0.2 m³, and its mass) or the refusal is rewritten. Screenshot: not copied into the repository because it shows the number; `~/.cache/lab-review/gasifier-lab/2026-11-11-efb36f2/evidence/spec-fuel-casing.png`. Likely file: `src/data/parts/dim-specs.ts` line 23 (`UNIT.bunkerR * 2`) and the form `lenM` in `src/data/lint.ts` (`printedForms` does not see « a × b m »).

**D2. The pilot's quantity is printed, which R4 refuses.** Where: `gas-train/pilot-injector`, Caractéristiques, « Gazole gardé · 15 à 20 % du gazole d'un diesel seul » (and the same line in the Aperçu); the spoken guide says it too (`part-pilot-injector`, « il garde de quinze à vingt pour cent du gazole d'un diesel seul »). Research A.2 R4 lists « pilot quantity » among what the lab does not give, and the sheet itself ends « Le lab ne donne ni quantité par coup, ni réglage ». The figure is reported (FAO) and it teaches « a diesel still needs a thread of diesel »; but it is, in plain words, how much pilot to keep. Either R4 is read as « per-stroke settings only » and says so, or the share goes. The same is true of the lines on process ratios, listed in D11. Screenshot: `docs/qa/d-pilot-sheet.jpg` (Aperçu tab; the row is on the Specs tab, text in `1280-fr/text.json`, key `gas-train/pilot-injector`).

**D3. Three parts under the bonnet cannot be read when selected: the extra governor, the injection pump, and in part the heat shield.** Select « Régulateur ajouté » in the tractor world: the camera comes close to the engine's grey flank and the part is an orange outline on it. The pump is an outline on the engine block. Nothing says « use Transparence ». In X-ray the governor is readable (a small valve with a lever). The 3D role knew (`docs/3d.md`, « Still weak », and `docs/kit-requests.md`: « X-ray while selected »). It is on a normal visit: a visitor who clicks the part list in the tractor world reaches these three. If the kit cannot do it, the lab can: ghost the block when one of the three is selected. Screenshots: `docs/qa/d-extra-governor-close.jpg`, `docs/qa/d-injection-pump-close.jpg`; also `1280-fr/tractor-extra-governor-{setting,mid}.png`.

**D4. The hot parts of the section are almost invisible in isolation and hard to tell from the field in the cut.** Where: gasifier world, `throat-ring`, `air-nozzles`, `air-intake`, `fire-box`, `grate`. In the cut they are glowing shapes of the same orange as the false-colour field around them (the nozzles are a row of faint teeth, the throat ring a pale wedge with a label pointing at it); isolated (state set from the store, the camera not moved), the nozzles, the intake and the grate are a few faint pixels on the cream ground. This is physically motivated (incandescence) and it is the lab's central subject: the visitor cannot see what a throat or a nozzle is. Screenshots: `docs/qa/d-hot-throat-ring.jpg`, `docs/qa/d-hot-air-nozzles.jpg`; isolated, state set from the store: `~/.cache/lab-review/gasifier-lab/2026-11-11-efb36f2/hot/iso-*.png` (the toolbar's own select-then-isolate flow was not run: see « Not checked »).

**D5. The strip's alarms are clipped in the garage at 1280.** Where: garage world, scenario « Le moteur s'arrête » or « Le garage fermé », 1280 × 800. The strip shows « Moteur arrêté : le gaz sort encore » and a second chip cut to « Dans… »; the two others, « Dans l'échelle des symptômes publiés » and « Le monoxyde a atteint la pièce du dessus », have no room (the DOM puts them at `x` beyond the viewport). They are the lab's most important alarms. The panel carries them, so no information is lost, but the strip is what the visitor reads while the clock runs. The kit lays the strip out, the lab raises three or four severe alarms at once: one fewer, or shorter texts, would fit. Screenshot: `docs/qa/d-strip-alarms-1280.jpg` (a crop of the strip is in the cache folder, `contact/strip-alarm.png`). Likely file: `src/panels/strip.ts` `stripAlarms`, `src/sim/texts.ts` `ALARM_TEXTS`.

**D6. Disposal wording on two sheets (R7), against the Sources panel's own promise.** Where: `farm/condensate-barrel` Aperçu (« Ce liquide ne doit rejoindre ni le sol, ni un égout, ni l'eau »), `farm/field-ditch` Aperçu (« Il est ici pour dire où l'eau noire ne doit pas aller : les condensats ne doivent atteindre ni le sol, ni les égouts, ni l'eau ») and its list line in the Parts catalogue (« l'endroit où l'eau noire ne doit pas aller »), and the ditch's Conception. R7: « no disposal advice »; the Sources panel says, in its list of refusals, « Aucun conseil sur le sort du goudron, des condensats et des cendres : ce lab dit ce qu'ils contiennent, et renvoie à vos autorités locales ». A prohibition is not a method, and it is what S2 says; but it is the nearest thing to guidance on the black water in the lab, it contradicts the promise a few clicks away, and the ditch exists to say it. Judgement for the lead: keep the hazard (what it holds, the ratio to the wartime sewer limit, « voyez votre autorité locale ») and drop « doit… ni le sol, ni l'égout, ni l'eau » and « où elle ne doit pas aller »; the ditch's job (« nothing is known of where it went ») stands without them. The narrator already keeps this out of the voice. Screenshots: `docs/qa/d-barrel-sheet.jpg`, `docs/qa/d-ditch-sheet.jpg`.

**D7. The spoken guide's own cues overwrite what the visitor set in the Simulation.** The narrator listed « resets of the sim at the start of some segments »; measured: 21 of the 73 segments carry a `lab:` cue, and in **16** of them (the introduction, `scene-gasifier`, `scene-gas-train`, `scene-garage`, and 12 parts) a visitor's load (55 %), moisture (22 %), ambient temperature (5 °C), door (ajar) and place (garage) were replaced by the defaults, with no word. In every segment that touches the simulation the end is the drawn machine (`lab:preset:mf-1100`), not what the visitor had: a visitor who sets a wet-chip run, then presses « Écouter » on any of these 12 parts, loses it. It is by design and a test holds it, and a button resets anyway, so this is a judgement more than a bug; but the intro is the first thing played, and a restore of the visitor's own state at the end of each segment would cost little. Data: `narr/narr-reset-fr.json`, `narr-reset-en.json`. Likely file: `src/cues.ts`.

## The lead's four questions

1. **Sheet `fuel-casing`**: a breach. D1.
2. **`condensate-barrel` and `field-ditch`**: wording that contradicts R7 and the Sources panel, though it is only a prohibition. D6.
3. **Resets of the simulation at the start of some segments**: by design and held by a test: but they also erase the visitor's own settings. D7. What the guide does leave on: nothing. After each of the 146 plays, x-ray, flows, explode, isolate and cut were off, the selection was the part just told (or none for the world and the introduction), the scenario was none and the simulation's parameters equalled a fresh `GasifierSim`'s (0 differences).
4. **The refusals as a reader sees them**: the Sources panel, the notice, the guides, the sheets and the voice kept what they say they keep, with the exceptions D1, D2, D6 and D11. Nothing reads as a procedure: no imperative of an operating verb in 1,627 text blocks (scan); the only imperatives are the interface's (« Lancez », « Ouvrez le monde ») and the quiz. The word list of research A.1 (« safe », « sûr », « sans danger »…) is absent outside the title of a cited document (« Final Guideline for Safe and Eco-friendly Biomass Gasification », « Safety Alert »), which the research allows. None of the sentences the lab says it does not repeat is repeated. No person is in the garage, no time left in a room is computed (the sim's rung line, below, is the nearest). The ladder sheet PL-G08 and the sim's line « Plus haut barreau publié atteint : … en 45 min, effondrement … en 2 h » put a published symptom-in-time sentence beside a running model figure: it is what R5 prescribes (the ladder beside the room), but a reader can read « the box stands on this rung » as « you would collapse in 2 hours »; the lead may want that line to name no time.

   Sizes of the hot parts as a reader finds them: I searched all 1,627 text blocks (the sheets in four tabs, the Sources, the guides, the lessons, the simulation panel, the SVG text of the ten sheets, both languages) for the 416 printed forms of the 26 withheld lengths (mm, cm, m, comma or point): **none found as such**. For any printed length within 1.2 % of one of them there are 68 hits, nearly all explained (the ditch, a drop in mm of water, the column's fall in cm a minute, the 3D envelopes). But the « Encombrement a × b × c » rows, which are measured on the 3D, repeat a withheld length to the millimetre in about twenty places; at least one is of the same kind as D1 (a part of the unit whose depth is the half of a drawn hot part). The rest look like chance. The list is in `refused-named-private.json` and `scan.py` outside the repository, because naming the parts here would print what they give away: the lead can ask for it.

   Does the model claim the machine « works »? No. The Sources panel says what it does not retrieve, the sheets mark « 55 kW against 51 » as worth little, the tractor sheet says the wood alone is not enough, and the model's flame is said never to go out. The two places that lean the other way are the intro's « il ne boit presque pas de gazole » (D17) and « un tracteur de cinq tonnes qui roule au bois ! » (`part-wood-gas-tractor`), softened a few words later.

## Verdict

The lab is complete against the bar, builds and tests clean, throws no console error at any width, and its simulation, sheets and spoken guide say what the model does not find with a candour rare for a first review: 146 playbacks of the guide followed their cues, left nothing on and restored the simulation. What stands between it and the owner is not breakage but the lab's own hard rules and three things a visitor meets at once. The rules: the fuel casing's sheet prints a size the lab says it withholds (D1), the pilot's share is printed under a rule that refuses it (D2), and two sheets say where the black water must not go against the Sources panel's promise (D6). What a visitor meets: three parts under the bonnet and the hot parts of the section cannot be seen when selected or isolated (D3, D4), and the garage's strip cuts off its most important alarms at 1280 (D5). I would not call it ready for the owner's checkpoint until the lead has settled D1, D2 and D6 (a few rows of text each) and decided on D3 and D4 (a ghost of the engine block, a brighter outline for the hot parts); with those done, the rest are minor and the owner could judge the voice. This is a recommendation, not a status: I have set none.
