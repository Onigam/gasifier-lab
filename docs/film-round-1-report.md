## 3. Shimmer and cost, measured

**The kit's flicker tool** (`docs/qa/film1-flicker.mjs`: the kit's own, with the lab's entry notice switched off, a modal that had been covering the middle of the 3D view and diluting every figure; 90 frames, a 5° orbit and a 20 % dolly on pinned clocks, the share of pixels whose change from frame to frame is not smooth), orbit / dolly, before (a copy of the tree before the round) → after (the working tree), same machine:

| World       | 1280 × 720                  | 390 × 844                   |
| ----------- | --------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| `farm`      | 2.27 / 5.64 → 2.26 / 5.69 % | 1.30 / 2.34 → 1.68 / 2.78 % |
| `tractor`   | 0.62 / 2.10 → 1.52 / 2.73 % | 0.59 / 1.92 → 0.90 / 2.07 % |
| `gasifier`  | 0.22 / 1.12 → 0.71 / 1.81 % | 0.50 / 2.53 → 1.58 / 3.62 % |
| `gas-train` | 0.45 / 1.25 → 0.48 / 1.77 % | 0.36 / 0.98 → 0.37 / 0.87 % |
| `garage`    | 0.54 / 2.36 → 1.90 / 5.65 % | 0.54 / 2.34 → 1.19 / 3.22 % |

**No pop** (the largest spike over the median frame is 1.63 at 390 px, 1.45 at 1280; a pop is over 2). **The machine worlds and the garage shimmer more than they did**: an open horizon, a gravel ground and a slab with its joints are edges the paper floor did not have. What I found by hiding objects one at a time and did about it: the tree line (1,100 small spruces) was the cause of the orbit's rise in `tractor` and `gasifier` (hidden: 2.56 → 1.16 and 1.54 → 0.42 in the 60-frame study); it is now 150 large ones, paler, from 380 m out, and the rise fell to 1.52 and 0.71; the slab's joints cost the gasifier's dolly about 0.5 and are now softer and gone from 16 m; every pattern finer than a pixel in the ground's shader is faded with the distance; the section's chips take the colour of their face from a few metres. What is left in `garage` is the house and the tractor, which my method could not hide. **`farm` is unchanged and the birches are not solved**: its trees are four fifths of its shimmer (all hidden: 3.39 → 0.80 and 8.16 → 1.14 in the 60-frame study) and the twelve birches about a fifth of its dolly; their coarse form beyond 30 m (the main limbs, fewer larger sprays of the same leaves) is in and tested and moved the figure by nothing. The kit's bar is a pop; the old figures of the machine worlds and the 1.6 % of the ram-pump site are not met by `garage` (1.9 % orbit) and `farm` (2.3 %). The film's own picture (8 samples a frame and the occlusion pass) is not smoother by this measure (`FILM=1`, 60 frames: farm 2.87 / 7.80, tractor 1.59 / 3.10, gasifier 0.96 / 2.62, gas-train 0.36 / 1.67, garage 2.42 / 7.66 %, against 2.84 / 7.59, 0.66 / 2.66, 0.28 / 1.57, 0.49 / 1.64, 0.50 / 3.23 before). Nobody has watched the lab at speed.

## 6. What is still wrong, and what I need from the other roles

1. **The farm is the lowest of the five** and its shimmer is unchanged (§3). The crowns are leaf cards and low cones, the walls a board map with no relief, the windows flat panes, the field a painted surface; no wheel tracks behind the tractor. The birches' level of detail (fewer, larger sprays and the main limbs only, beyond 30 m) is in and tested but did not move the figure: my reading, not tested: a card whose coverage sits at the alpha test's edge toggles with every move; the fix would be in the material (alpha sharpened per mip level, or a lower test at a distance), which changes every crown, and I did not get to it.
2. **The Sankey on the floor of `gas-train` is flat colour** (by design: no figures, no text); it is a third of the frame at the line's home shot. A procedural grain and a soft edge would take the aplat off it; not done.
3. **The cylinder's cut face is flat white** (the section's pale colour, as the walls'): a drafting hatch would be the convention; not done.
4. **The bedroom is box furniture**, the plume is sprites, the yard of `garage` has no props; the house stands on the side of the garage opposite to the farm's (so that the home view does not look through it). Seen from the film's camera at the home pose nothing shows it; seen from the other side the house is hidden.
5. **The shimmer of the machine worlds and the garage rose** (§3): an open horizon, a gravel ground and a slab with joints are edges that the paper did not have. What I did (every fine pattern faded with the distance, a tree line of 420 trees and not 1,100, the chips fading into the face from a few metres) and what is left is in §3.
6. **Not looked at**: a real phone, Safari, Firefox, the lab at 60 frames a second, the guide, the lessons, the drawings, the three widths of the interface (only the 3D at 390 px in the flicker tool), the explode of the tractor against its new contact patches beyond one still (`tractor-8-explode`).
7. **The film's driver** needs from the lab only what `docs/3d.md` lists (« Hooks for the film's driver »). It has no `films/gasifier-lab/lab.json` yet.
8. **Needed from other roles**: the physicist, request 7 (the analyser's last point reads the model's end of the reduction); the data role, a part id for the lid in `garage` if the film wants to name it; the lead, request 9 (the showcase card) and, if the film is to say « our evening », the address with `?hour=`.
