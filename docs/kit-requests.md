# Kit requests (what `@onigam/lab-kit` 0.6.0 lacks, from the physics of Gasifier Lab)

Numbered, one paragraph each. Nothing here was worked around by patching the kit; each item says what the lab did instead.

1. **`stackDraft` and pressure.** `stackDraft(H, Tin, Tbeside)` reads `airRho` at sea level, with no argument for the pressure (known to the lead). This lab uses it once, for the draught through a lid that does not seal (`Plant.lidLeakFlow`), on a farm in southern Sweden, so at sea level and at an estimated leak the error does not matter here. Asked: an optional `p` on `stackDraft`, as `airRho` already has. Nothing to do in this lab when it comes.
2. **`tubeFlow` for a reversed flow.** Fixed on `~/Dev/lab-kit` at `2680d9e`, not released in 0.6.0. This lab only passes positive flows (`pipeDrop` in `train.ts`, and it returns 0 first when nothing flows), so no figure moves. Bump when released; no change here.
3. **A small `sim/gas`, when a second lab needs it.** `src/sim/gas/` holds what research §5.6 foresaw: heating values and stoichiometric air of a mixture, mixture energy, dew point (`species.ts`), the one-equilibrium, four-balance method with its energy balance (`equilibrium.ts`), and a pair of well-mixed rooms for a toxic gas with its span (`room.ts`). No lab's name, nothing imported from outside the folder, no allocation in the hot path; `gas.test.ts` holds it against closed forms and FAO's own curves. The biodigester has a `bio/gas.ts` of its own for wet biogas: the room and the mixture energy could serve it (H₂S, CH₄), the mass heater could use the room for CO. It stays here until the lead decides.
4. **A strip value and a readout with a tag.** The solar lab's request 4, the biodigester's 4. `StripValue` has `label`, `value`, `unit`. `stripValues` here carries a `tag` of † or * that changes with the state. For now the lead can append it to the unit. Asked: `tag?: string` on `StripValue` and on `readout`.
5. **Which values on which world.** `LabStrip.values(sim)` does not receive the active world. Research §5.5 asks for carbon monoxide and minutes since the stop on `garage`, and the fuel figures elsewhere. What the lab did: `stripValues` switches on `state.place === 'garage'`, so a scenario in the garage shows the room's figures on every world, and a visitor who opens `garage` with the tractor still working in the field sees the field's. Asked: `values(sim, activeScene)`.
6. **A logarithmic axis on a chart.** Sheet 8 and the room's panel put 20 ppm and 270,000 ppm on one scale, and a room's rise from 0 to 3,500 ppm beside a ladder starting at 20. `LineChart` and `CurveChart` read linear. What the lab can do meanwhile: plot `log10(ppm + 1)` and print its own ticks, which is what the author's drawing does anyway. Asked: `yScale: 'log'` with decades as ticks.
7. **A band on a line chart.** `garageCoPpmLow` and `garageCoPpmHigh` frame `garageCoPpm`: the honest way to show that ventilation is a guess. `LineChart` draws lines. What the lab can do: three lines, the outer two thin. Asked: a filled band between two series.
8. **The strip's values past the sixth, and a long clock.** The biodigester's 13, Mill's 8. This lab's working strip has nine values, and `strip.ts` orders them so that the first six are the ones to keep, which is not research §5.5's order. The speed runs to ×3600 and a working day to eight hours: `×3600` says little to a visitor.

## From the 3D (`lab-modeler`), kit 0.6.0

9. **A part that moves by itself cannot be registered as it is.** `PartRegistry` puts a part back at its base position every frame (the explode), so a tractor that drives round a field (`farm`) or a crusher it tows would be dragged back to the origin. What the lab did: the registered group stays still, and the vehicle is a child of it that `update` moves (`tractorPart` and `drive` in `src/three/farm/scene.ts`). Asked: a flag on `registry.add` (`moves: true`) that leaves a part's position to the world, or a note in `docs/3d.md` of the kit.
10. **Selecting a part that moves.** The camera frames the part where it stands when it is clicked, and the tractor then drives away. What the lab did: the tractor and its crusher stand while one of them is selected, so the world takes the selection (`selected: () => string | null`, to be wired by `src/lab.ts`) and a film's `[[frame:wood-gas-tractor]]` finds it still. Asked: `stage.follow(id)` for the camera, or a `SceneModule.update` that receives the selection.
11. **The camera in `update`.** `garage` is cut open by the walls between the eye and the room stepping aside; `SceneModule.update(state, dt, view)` has no camera, so the world does it in `scene.onBeforeRender`. It works and is the one place where a world reads the camera. Asked: `view.camera` (position and direction) in `ViewOpts`, as the sand lab's and the mass heater's requests already did.
12. **A picture that holds only a plume.** `Points` cannot be clicked, and a part with no mesh has no box on its sheet. `post-gas-plume` and `exhaust-plume` carry an invisible haze mesh (opacity 0) round the plume so that they can be selected and framed. Asked: `Points` in `PartRegistry.pickables()`, or a `pickProxy` on a part.
13. **X-ray around a part that is inside another.** `injection-pump` and `heat-shield` stand under the bonnet; selecting them in the normal view shows an outline, not the part. What the lab did: the bonnet is not a part, it is a follower of `diesel-engine` that goes to the ghost whenever X-ray is on or the machine is exploded (`Followers.force`). Since then the bonnet opens to the ghost when the part selected is one of those under it, and they are looked at from above their open side: they still read as an outline beside the engine's block. Asked: `SceneDef.xrayWhenSelected?: (partId) => string[]` (the parts to turn transparent when this one is selected: the engine's block as well as the bonnet), which would let the pump be seen without X-ray.
14. **The overview of a world that changes size.** `garage` redraws its room when the model's volume goes from 50 to 1,000 m³ (the walls move 10 m); the camera does not follow. Asked: `stage.frameAll()` called by the kit when a world says its extent changed (`module.extentChanged()`), or `LabSim.onChange` reaching the stage.
15. **`registry.bounds` counts every vertex of a `Points`.** The kit measures a part with `Box3.setFromObject(o, true)`, which reads each vertex of what the part holds, a `Points` object included, whatever its `geometry.boundingBox` says and whether or not it is in the draw range. Dots of « Flux » parked far away (a hidden dot 1,000 m under the world) made the overview and the explode frame the void. What the lab did: a dot that is not shown has no size (a shader attribute) and waits on its path's start; a test holds the lowest point of every world with « Flux » on. Asked: `bounds` that skips `Points`, or an `object.userData.noBounds`, so that no lab has to learn it from a blank screen.

## From the integration (`lab-lead`), kit 0.6.0

16. **A notice on the way in.** Research Q5 (the owner's « oui ») asks the warning to be shown once on entering, before anything can be pressed. The kit has the invitation of the spoken guide and nothing that blocks. What the lab did (`src/panels/entry.ts`): a native `<dialog>` opened with `showModal()` at mount, holding the card of the kit's « no WebGL » page (`unsupported__card`, `panel-title`, `lead`, `callout--warn`, `btn--primary`) so that no colour, font or layout is the lab's, and one line of inline style to take away the browser's own box around that card (`padding:0;border:0;background:transparent`, a maximum size). Remembered in `localStorage` per wording (`gasifier-lab:entry-notice`). It is the one place where the lab writes a style. Asked: `LabDefinition.notice?: { title: L; body: L[]; accept: L; version: string }`, shown by the frame once per browser and per version, in the frame's own look, with the key `Esc` and focus handled, and left out of a film's capture by a query the film sets. Until then a walker (`lab-kit/tools/review/walk.mjs`) must press its button before it can click anything, or set `localStorage` first.
17. **A spoken guide that is not there yet.** `narration.manifest: 'audio/{lang}/manifest.json'` makes the kit ask for a file that does not exist, and a lab without audio logs a 404 in the console on every load, which a review that counts console errors cannot tell from a defect. What the lab did: the function form returns `null` until the narrator flips `SPOKEN` in `src/lab.ts`, and `src/lab.test.ts` holds that the function answers a path exactly where `public/audio/<lang>/manifest.json` exists. Asked: a quiet probe in the kit (an ordinary `fetch` whose 404 is caught), or the function form in `docs/narration.md`.
18. **Requests 4 to 7 of the physics, as the lead wired them.** (4) The mark of a value follows its unit in the strip (`† ` or `*` after `kW`), until `StripValue` has `tag`. (5) `onFrame` tells `src/panels/strip.ts` which world is open: the garage shows the room's figures, a dash where the model has nothing yet (no carbon monoxide has been in the box, nothing has stopped), never a nought. (6) The room's chart plots `log10(ppm)` on the kit's linear axis, from 1 ppm to a million, so its four ticks fall on 1, 100, 10,000 and 1,000,000. (7) The span is three lines, the two outer ones dashed. None of the four is a defect of the lab; each wants the kit's own answer.
19. **The strip's alarm is one line, and the top bar has a fixed share.** (a) An alarm's label is cut with an ellipsis at 1280 px and wraps into three or four tiny lines at 768 px, over its neighbours; the simulation's sentences are written for the panel (« Moteur arrêté : plus d'aspiration, le gaz continue de se former et sort de l'appareil »). What the lab did: a short form of each alarm for the strip (`STRIP_ALARMS` in `src/panels/strings.ts`, about 40 characters), the sentence kept in the panel. Asked: a `detail` on `StripAlarm` that the kit shows on hover, press or in the panel, and an ellipsis at every width. (b) With five worlds the lab's name is cut to « Labo du… » at 768 px (the scene switch takes the room), and at 390 px, with the switch scrolling, the active world can stand out of the visible part of the scroller (« Le garage », the last of five). Asked: scroll the active world into view when it changes, and give the name its room before the switch.

## From the independent review (`lab-reviewer`), kit 0.6.0

20. **A narration session that saves the visitor's simulation settings at its start and restores them at its end.** 21 of the spoken guide's 73 segments carry a `lab:` cue (`src/cues.ts`), and in 16 of them (the introduction, the gasifier, the gas line and the garage, and 12 parts) the visitor's load, moisture, ambient temperature, door and place were replaced by the defaults with no word, and the segment ends on the drawn machine (`lab:preset:mf-1100`), not on what the visitor had: someone who sets a wet-chip run and then presses « Écouter » on one of those parts loses it. It is by design and a test holds it, and a button resets anyway, so this is a judgement more than a defect; but the introduction is the first thing played. What the lab did: nothing yet. Asked: the kit saves the simulation's parameters when a segment or the guided tour starts and puts them back when it ends or is stopped, or gives the lab a `narration.onStart` and `onEnd` to do it.
21. **The lab's title at 768 px.** The top bar leaves the lab's title eight pixels at 768 px (`scrollWidth` 50 against `clientWidth` 8): only the icon and a « 5 » show, in French and in English; at 1280 and 390 it reads. It is the same squeeze as 19 (b), seen on the title. What the lab did: nothing; shortening `brand.title` would be its fallback if the frame cannot give the title room. Asked: the title keeps a minimum width before the worlds' switch takes the rest, or ends in an ellipsis.
22. **The strip at 390 and 768 px.** At 390 px the strip's cells are cut at the edge (« Gazole 2,2 kg/h » reads « Gazol »), and the scene tabs and the toolbar scroll sideways with « La ligne de gaz » half cut; at 768 px the strip's content is 779 px wide in a 768 px screen. All of it is the frame's layout, reported once. Asked: a visible edge or a wrap for the cells at 390, and a strip that fits 768.

## From the film round (`lab-modeler`, 2026-11-16), kit 0.6.0

23. **A part's frame that follows what it frames.** `PartFraming.frame` is a box taken once, at `registry.add` (cloned). The tractor of `farm` drives round a field and its frame is its own box where it stands now; the plume of `garage` frames the cloud at the intake, and with a lid that leaks the lid too. What the lab did: it writes into `registry.get(id).frame` every frame (the field `Part.frame` is public and `frameBounds` reads it each call). Asked: `frame` may be a function of the registry (`() => Box3`), or `registry.setFrame(id, box)`, so that nobody has to know the field is read live.
24. **A way for a film to ask a world for something the model does not carry.** The farm's sun follows the model's clock and holds the early afternoon over ×600; a film wants an evening for its first shot. What the lab did: the world reads `?hour=19.5` in the address (once) and `scene.userData.filmHour` (live), documented in `docs/3d.md`. Asked: a `film` field on `SceneModule` (a bag of named options the film's driver sets, `module.film?.set('hour', 19.5)`), so that the driver does not reach into `scene.userData` and every lab does not invent its own address.
25. **Ambient occlusion that leaves foliage out.** The film's occlusion pass (`Stage.setLook({ ao: true })`) darkens the inside of every crown of leaf cards as it would a corner; `userData.look.aoRadius` is a world's only handle. Not verified on a film render (no film of this lab has been rendered). Asked: a flag on a material or an object that keeps it out of the occlusion's normal and depth pass, as the lab's own `hills` and `shadows` are in the house lab.

## From the film round 2 (`lab-modeler`, 2026-11-17), kit 0.6.0

26. **`patchMaterial` should keep a patched material out of the merge.** `mergeStatic` bakes every plain `MeshStandardMaterial` of a shape into one white, vertex-coloured material, and a material that carries a shader patch (the section's hatch) looks plain to it: the merged faces kept the colour and lost the patch (the cylinder's cut walls had no hatch while its piston and rod, which move and are not merged, had it). What the lab did: its own `merge.ts` honours `userData.noShare`, and `hatch` sets it. Asked: that `patchMaterial` set `userData.noShare = true` itself (or that the kit's own merge test `customProgramCacheKey`), so that the next lab does not find it by looking at a still.
27. **A reading of the frame that a film's flight sweeps.** The flight of the kit (`flyTo`) is a straight line of the camera and its target, eased; a world that steps walls aside by the eye's place cannot know where the eye will pass. What the lab did: its walls step aside with a clearance of half a metre and a test walks the same line between two framings (`film.test.ts`). Asked: `Stage.flightPath(ids)` (or an event with the poses of a flight before it starts) so that a world can hide what the camera will cross, without copying the kit's pose arithmetic into a test.

## From the film round 3 (`lab-modeler`, 2026-11-18), kit 0.6.0

28. **An overview that is what the world says, by the shape of the view.** `Stage.frameAll` frames the union of every part's box along `home`'s direction: in a landscape whose parts run on (80 m of ditch, a column of gas 9 m high) the farm was a small thing in a strip at 768 and 390 px, and the lab could not say « frame this ». What the lab did: `WorldRegistry.overview(extent, alone)` cuts the box of every part that is not the selected one to the overview's own boxes, and the farm's `home`, `homePadding` and overview box are getters that read the camera's aspect (seen in `scene.onBeforeRender`). Asked: `SceneModule.overview?: (aspect: number) => { box: Box3; direction: Vector3; padding?: number }`, used by `frameAll` instead of the union when it is given.
29. **An overview aimed at a point.** `fitBox` aims at the middle of the box and sets the distance from its sphere. A line of machines with a diagram on the ground in front of it is not seen about the middle of its box: the gas line's camera stood a fifth further than it needed to. What the lab did: its wide overview is its box stretched until the middle is the point to aim at (`homeWide` in `gastrain/scene.ts`), with the padding that puts the eye where it should be. Asked: `home` taken as a pose when the world says so (`homeExact: true`), or the `overview` of request 28 with an `aim`.
30. **The Chrome lock is not fair.** `with-chrome-lock.sh` polls every ten seconds; a job started the moment another ends takes the lock before every waiter, and a waiter can starve for an hour while short jobs follow one another (this round waited 40 minutes for one batch). Not the kit's, the video repository's: said here because every lab's measurements go through it. Asked: a queue (a numbered ticket in the lock directory, served in order).
