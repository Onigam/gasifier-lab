## 3. A size that equalled a refused one, printed on a sheet

The spec « Diamètre × hauteur de la soute » of `fuel-casing` printed the bunker's diameter, which is, to the centimetre, the lower casing's outer diameter that `GASIFIER_REFUSED` withholds (the review's D1). The voice never said it (it says the bunker's volume in litres and the chips' mass). The row is gone (`15e7225`). The number is not written here: R2 reaches this file too.

The sentences are gone from the sheets (`docs/data.md`); the voice never said them: it says what the water holds, the ratio to the wartime sewer limit, and « voyez votre autorité locale », and `field-ditch` is told as a question (« où allait l'eau noire ? ») that nothing the lab read answers.

## 5. Read in summary, then read: what the voice still leaves out

The fact-check has now read S7 in full and the other sources, so none of these stands on a source read in summary. They stay out of the voice for the budget and for the once-only rule, and each can be added at about 200 characters if the lead wants it, in the fact-check's wording:

- The nine deaths in pellet stores (a British alert of 2012, sealed pellet stores, since 2002): the voice says only that pellets kept in a closed store give off carbon monoxide.
- The French limits of 20 and 100 ppm and the alarms of 25 and 50 ppm (`co-indicator`).
- The 17 deaths of 1939 to 1941 (an American handbook citing a 1983 work) and the 155 faulty gasifiers of 230 (checked after police reports): the voice says the fire count and the first quarter of 1942.
